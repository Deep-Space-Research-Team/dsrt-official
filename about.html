About Our Team

Our Mission

The Deep Space Research Team is a dedicated collective of engineers, researchers, and developers pushing the boundaries of celestial observation and data processing. We build open-source APIs and web platforms for tracking Near-Earth Objects (NEOs).

Our Technology

We leverage modern web frameworks, scalable cloud infrastructure, and robust API architectures to distribute real-time telemetry and orbital mechanics data to researchers around the globe.
