# 🛰️ D.S.R.T. — Deep Space Research Team

### *Exploring Space. Engineering Discovery.*

D.S.R.T. (Deep Space Research Team) is an independent space technology and research initiative focused on developing software, web systems, and open-source tools that make space-related data more accessible and useful.

We explore the intersection of **space science, software engineering, data analysis, and modern web technologies** to develop projects that support the exploration and understanding of our universe.

🌐 **Official Website:** https://dsrt-official.netlify.app/  
💻 **GitHub Organization:** https://github.com/Deep-Space-Research-Team

---

## 🌌 Our Mission

Our mission is to build accessible, reliable, and innovative digital tools for space-data exploration and research.

We aim to combine engineering and scientific curiosity to make complex space-related information easier to discover, process, analyse, and understand.

We believe that open-source software and collaborative development can help make space technology more accessible to developers, researchers, students, and space enthusiasts.

## 🔭 Areas of Focus

### 🛰️ Space Data & Telemetry
Exploring software systems and data workflows for collecting, organising, processing, and analysing space-related information.

### ☄️ Near-Earth Objects (NEOs)
Investigating ways to present and analyse information about asteroids, comets, and other objects that approach Earth's orbital region.

### 🌍 Celestial Mechanics & Orbital Analysis
Developing computational approaches and software tools for understanding celestial motion, orbital parameters, and trajectory-related data.

### ⚙️ APIs & Data Infrastructure
Building web services, data-processing workflows, and developer-friendly interfaces that can support space-related applications.

### 💻 Open-Source Technology
Experimenting with modern programming languages, web technologies, automation, and software architecture to create useful public-facing projects.

## 🧪 Our Principles

- **Scientific curiosity:** Ask meaningful questions and explore ideas through evidence and experimentation.
- **Open development:** Share code and technical knowledge when project licenses and applicable restrictions permit.
- **Transparency:** Document data sources, assumptions, limitations, and known issues.
- **Responsible engineering:** Prioritize reliability, maintainability, and appropriate handling of data.
- **Continuous improvement:** Learn from testing, feedback, collaboration, and experimentation.

## 🤝 Contributing

We welcome constructive contributions from developers, students, researchers, and space enthusiasts interested in relevant projects.

You can get involved by:

1. Exploring our public repositories.
2. Reporting reproducible bugs or suggesting improvements through GitHub Issues, where available.
3. Contributing code, documentation, tests, or research-oriented tools.
4. Discussing ideas and proposing practical new features.

Before contributing, review the selected repository's README, contribution instructions, and license.

## 🛠️ Technology

Depending on the project, our work may involve:

- Web development and modern JavaScript
- APIs and data-processing systems
- Scientific computing and numerical analysis
- Data visualisation and automation
- Open-source development tools

The actual technology stack and requirements are documented in each individual repository.

## 📡 Research & Data Responsibility

Space-data applications should communicate their sources, limitations, and uncertainty clearly.

Any analytical output produced by a DSRT project should be interpreted according to its documentation and validated against appropriate authoritative sources when necessary. Experimental tools should not be treated as substitutes for official space-agency alerts or operational guidance.
